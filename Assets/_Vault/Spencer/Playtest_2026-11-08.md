# Play-test: one session, riskiest first

Distilled from six verification files with about 190 open boxes between them. These 39 checks are the ones most likely to be broken: never played, touched recently, or spanning several systems. Tick here as you go; when something fails, note what you saw next to it (a screenshot in `Tasks/Claude/` helps) and Claude will chase it and tick the matching box in the full file.

**Before you start:** restart the Editor once (clears the stale Burst JIT cache), then enter Play so the subscenes rebake.

## 1. Caravan on the terrain (`Scenes/TerrainWorld.unity`), changed today
- [ ] Drive across three or more tiles at normal zoom: the wheels stay on the ground over hills and dips; nothing floats or sinks.
- [ ] Zoom all the way out, drive fast across several tiles, zoom back in: the caravan is on the ground (the flying-caravan fix). A short pause at a tile edge while it loads is expected.
- [ ] Drive up a steep hill: it rolls over the slope; when blocked it can still pivot away.
- [ ] Shadows point south, toward the camera, and don't move as the clock runs. Do the characters read too dark lit from behind? (`sunElevationDegrees` / `sunLightTravelYawDegrees` on `WorldAtmosphereDriver`.)
- [ ] Walk the player across a tile border: no stutter, falling or sticking, no console spam.

## 2. Caravan basics (`Scenes/CampLoop.unity` from here on): never played
- [ ] E at a side or the front seats you and switches the controls to Vehicle; E at the back opens the workstation menu.
- [ ] Mounting the workstation puts you inside on the floor (not falling through), with the back doors open so you can see yourself. Move `CaravanSim/MountPoint` in the prefab to choose the spot.
- [ ] Dismount (Tab): you walk to the tailgate, hop down behind the caravan, and the doors close after you.
- [ ] Summon a Brute while you're inside: the doors still shut, shake and slam open; afterwards they stay open while you're inside.
- [ ] Driving feels like the old VehicleController: heads where you push, slow pull-away, lazy wide turns, no reverse.
- [ ] Drive into a wall: it stops dead and doesn't clip through. You can turn away.
- [ ] E at speed is refused; stopped, E puts you down beside the front left.
- [ ] Full speed through bandits: they're flung, land, lie 1–2 s and get up; the caravan slows per hit. Under 2 m/s they're only pushed.
- [ ] Summon a Zombie: it walks out of the back and hops down. Summon a Brute: doors shut, shake, doors slam open, smoke, brute in the smoke.
- [ ] Furnace on with wood: the chimney smokes, parked and driving.

## 3. Camp loop core: never played since 2026-10-13
- [ ] HUD reads `Bodies 2 · Wood 10 · Scrap 5 · Electricity 5 · Rounds 3 · Currency 0`.
- [ ] Field revive: kill a citizen, slot 1, use on the corpse; a zombie rises and Rounds −1.
- [ ] Mount the caravan's work table: furnace (Wood −1, Electricity +2 every ~5 s), craft a round, summon each type (cost leaves at once, bar fills, unit obeys orders).
- [ ] Squads: select, press 1 or 2 to assign; Q radial issues Follow / Attack / Return / Hold / Gather.
- [ ] Right-click a tree with a selected unit: they harvest and carry wood back; Wood climbs.
- [ ] Bandit camp (~30 m north): they attack within ~12 m, give up past ~25 m; clearing the camp unlocks the crate and strongbox.
- [ ] At 0 HP: the knock-out overlay counts down and you wake at the caravan.
- [ ] Save, change counts, load: the counts come back.

## 4. Trade: tested in code, never seen
- [ ] HUD shows `Week 1 · Mon 06:00`, and the hour ticks about once a real minute; the pause menu stops it.
- [ ] The pigeon lands on the arm, the letter opens, the mouse cursor appears, and Accept, Decline and Close are clickable.
- [ ] Keyboard: Submit accepts, E closes, Esc opens the pause menu over the letter. Closing lets time run and the pigeon flies off.
- [ ] The letter arriving while you drive the caravan still opens, and closing it puts you back in the seat.
- [ ] Accept an order: a yellow pole appears at the drop-off. Are the six drop-offs in sensible places?
- [ ] Bring the ordered units to the pole and press E: they leave the squad and Currency goes up. With the caravan parked beside the pole, which one gets the E?
- [ ] **Skip day** past the due day: the order fails with −15 reputation.

## 5. Smart AI (H1–H5)
- [ ] Bandits walk between camp waypoints and sit, visit the crate, or gather at the gramophone.
- [ ] One bandit engaging you pulls in the others within ~15 m.
- [ ] An unarmed bandit picks up a rock and throws it from 3–12 m; walking up close makes it back off and then throw.
- [ ] Every throw and shot has a visible wind-up (swell + white flash + sound), and side-stepping dodges it. Does the flash read on the pale bodies?
- [ ] The rifle bandit shoots from range; a careless shooter hits a friend standing in the line.
- [ ] A bandit below ~35 % runs to a bandage and heals.

## 6. Living humans: the owner's-eye pass only

- [ ] Within a few minutes someone visits the Bar, shows a mug, then weaves and shows green bubbles.
- [ ] A drunk brawl never takes the victim below about a third of its health, and nobody turns on you because of it.
